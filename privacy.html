Privacy Policy

Passport Photo: ID & Visa · Last updated 30 September 2026

In short: your photos never leave your iPhone. We don't collect your name, email or any personal details; purchases are recorded under a random ID only. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Photos

Photos you take or choose are processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. That processing covers face detection, background replacement and the requirement checks. Photos are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Photos you unlock are stored in the app's private storage on your device so you can export them again. You can delete them at any time in My Photos or Settings → Delete All My Photos. Deleting the app also deletes them. If you use iCloud or device backups, they may be included in your own backups under Apple's terms.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only to take your photo. The app can only add images to your photo library, and only when you tap Save. It cannot read your library. When you pick a photo, iOS gives the app only the photo you selected.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to your purchase.

To keep track of purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. When you buy, RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase record (what was bought, when, and the price) together with a random identifier that the app creates on your device. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and RevenueCat never receives your photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we collect

Only the purchase records described above, used to deliver what you bought. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and apart from purchases it makes no network requests. Your photos are never collected.

Children

The app can be used to make photos of children for their documents. No data about anyone photographed is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
